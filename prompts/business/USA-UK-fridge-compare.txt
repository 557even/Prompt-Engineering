+===================================================================================================================+
|                         NOTES & CONVERSION FACTS                                                               |
+===================================================================================================================+

1. **Capacity conversion** – 1 cu ft = 28.3 L.  A 30‑cu‑ft US fridge ≈ 850 L, which is roughly double the typical UK family unit.
2. **Energy‑cost conversion** – 2024 average residential electricity price:
   • USA: $0.14 /kWh (EIA)
   • UK: £0.34 /kWh → $0.43 /kWh (based on £1 ≈ $1.27, average 2024 exchange)
3. **Price conversion** – £1 ≈ $1.29 (average 2024).  Prices shown are list‑price ranges; actual in‑store price may vary with promotions, delivery, and installation.
4. **Voltage considerations** – US fridges operate on 115 V/60 Hz; a UK home will need a 230 V unit.  Plug adapters are NOT a solution for the voltage difference.
5. **Regulatory notes** – US Energy‑Star certification requires ≤ 560 kWh/yr for a 30‑cu‑ft fridge‑freezer.  EU Energy‑Label A++ corresponds to ≤ 260 kWh/yr for a 340‑L fridge‑freezer.
6. **Typical brand models** (2024 best‑sellers):
   • **US**: Whirlpool WRS325SDHZ, GE GSS25GSHSS, Samsung RF28R7351SG, Frigidaire FFSS2611US.
   • **UK**: Samsung RB31FERNDSA, LG GBB68PZL, Bosch KGN39VLEA, Beko RTG56051WB.
7. **Feature trends** – Smart‑home integration (Wi‑Fi, app‑controlled temperature) is now common in US mid‑range models; the UK market is slower but growing, with more “connected” units appearing in 2024‑25.
